# About Trident Films

HTML version: https://www.tridentfilms.ca/about

Cinematic wedding films for couples in Winnipeg, Brandon, Regina and beyond, made by a small team of storytellers who have filmed more than 120 love stories.

## Who we are

Trident Films is a wedding videography team that films cinematic wedding films for couples across Manitoba, Saskatchewan and beyond. We think of ourselves as storytellers first and videographers second. Our job on your wedding day is to capture authentic emotion, and our job afterwards is to shape that footage into a film you will want to watch for generations.

Our team has filmed over 120 wedding films across six years, from intimate backyard weddings and private farms to golf courses, galleries and destination celebrations. Recent films include The Kilmurys at Pineridge Hollow, Mackenzie & Lindsay at a private farm, Michael & Serena Gawryluk at Bel Acres Golf & Country Club, The Rosnoskis at the Winnipeg Art Gallery, and The LeBlancs at Bridges Golf Course.

## Where we work

Winnipeg and surrounding communities (Headingley, Oak Bluff, Selkirk, Steinbach); Brandon and the Westman region; Regina and across Saskatchewan; destination weddings.
