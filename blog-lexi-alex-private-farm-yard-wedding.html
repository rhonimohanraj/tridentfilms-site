Lexi & Alex: Vows About Everyday Life, Filmed in Winnipeg

We filmed Lexi and Alex's vintage-vibes wedding day at a private farm yard in Winnipeg. The part of the film that stays with us most is Lexi's vow to Alex, which spends its time on the small, shared routines of their life together. Here is what we heard in it.

October 15, 2026·2 min read

Vows made of ordinary days

Lexi begins by telling Alex how proud she is of the person he has become, and how special, loved and thought of he makes her feel every single day. From there she names the life they have built together and then lists what she wants to keep doing: grocery shopping together, going to the gym together, putting the clean sheets on their bed together, cooking supper together.

She even mentions rearranging their tired days just so they can drive into the city together. The list is specific and a little funny, and it tells you exactly how the two of them live.

"I love the life we've built together."

A promise she calls the easiest one

Lexi says the easiest promise she can make is to never stop laughing and loving life with Alex. She calls him the absolute best teammate in life and says that together they can take on the world. She ends with a simple 'I love you.'

Right after the officiant pronounces them husband and wife, we hear Lexi say, 'And to my husband, we did it.'

Friends who have known them for years

The officiant reminds Lexi and Alex that they have known each other since high school. The first spark was Alex seeing Lexi in the hallway outside the gym and wondering who that cute little thing was. The officiant also speaks about watching them grow, spend time finding themselves as individuals and come back to one another.

A letter read in the film speaks of gratitude, and of having just as much fun with the little mundane things as with traveling the world or completely gutting and renovating a house. A toast closes the film with a raised glass to two people and two families, and a friend saying how excited they are for the next chapter.

"Let's promise to never stop grocery shopping together, going to the gym together, putting the clean sheets on our bed together, cooking supper together"

What to take from it

The vows people remember are often the ones that name real routines. If you are planning a wedding at a private farm yard near Winnipeg or a similar setting, Lexi's list of everyday promises is a good example of how personal a vow can feel.
