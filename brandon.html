Filming a wedding in the Wheat City.

A Brandon wedding often moves between very different places in one day: a family home in the morning, a church or a riverside ceremony in the afternoon, and a hotel ballroom or community hall at night. We plan the film around those moves, so the crew is already set up when the moments happen.

We take weddings at Brandon venues such as the Keystone Centre, the Victoria Inn Hotel & Convention Centre, Royal Oak Inn & Suites, the Riverbank Discovery Centre and Brandon University, as well as halls, farms and family land across Westman.

Keystone Centre
A large complex with several halls. We confirm your room, the head table and dance floor layout, and the sound setup for speeches before the day.
Victoria Inn and Royal Oak Inn
Hotel venues where getting ready, the reception and sometimes the ceremony share one building, which keeps the timeline tight and leaves more time for portraits.
Riverbank Discovery Centre
Set on the Assiniboine River trails, with open sky and water close by for an outdoor ceremony or a walk between the ceremony and dinner.
Brandon University
A campus setting for couples with a connection to the school, with older buildings that give portraits some texture.

Portraits, light and getting around Brandon.

Our favourite Brandon portrait spots are Rideau Park for its tree canopy and pond, the Daly House Museum for Victorian brick and gardens, the rolling grassland of the Brandon Hills, and the brick storefronts along Rosser Avenue downtown. We wrote about each one in our guide to wedding film locations in Brandon and Winnipeg.

Getting-ready footage happens wherever you are: a family home in the South End, Westridge or Valleyview, a house on the North Hill, an acreage out toward the Brandon Hills, or a hotel suite near downtown. We map the drive between each stop ahead of time so the day keeps its pace.

In late June the sun sets in Brandon close to 10 p.m., a little later than in Winnipeg, so sunset portraits usually fit between dinner and the first dance. For fall and winter weddings we plan them for mid-afternoon instead.

Brandon wedding video questions.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Brandon?

Our Essential Collection is $4,399 plus GST. It covers 10 hours with two videographers and includes a teaser, a 5 to 6 minute highlight film, and full ceremony and toasts films. A $1,500 retainer holds your date, and the balance is split into two payments, three months and fifteen days before the wedding. Travel outside Brandon is itemized on your quote.

How long until we receive our wedding film?

Your complete film gallery arrives 16 to 20 weeks after the wedding. Each film is edited, colour graded and scored with licensed music, then delivered together in a private online showcase you can share with family. If anything would push delivery past 20 weeks, we tell you ahead of time and explain why.

Do you film at Keystone Centre?

Yes. We take weddings at the Keystone Centre and at other Brandon venues. The complex has several halls, so during planning we confirm which room you have, where the head table and dance floor sit, and how speeches are amplified, so the ceremony and toasts audio comes through clean in your films.

Is drone footage allowed at Brandon venues?

Often, with planning. Drone flights depend on the venue's permission, the weather, and Transport Canada rules, including extra limits close to Brandon Municipal Airport on the north side of the city. Rural venues and family land across Westman usually have the most room. Ask about aerial coverage when you inquire and we will check your location first.

Do you travel outside Brandon?

Yes. We film across Westman, including Virden, Souris, Neepawa, Minnedosa, Carberry, Shilo, Boissevain and Dauphin, and on farms and acreages in between. Travel is listed on its own line of your quote so you can see it clearly. We also film in Winnipeg and Regina.

Can we book video with photography, a DJ or a photo booth?

Yes. Trident Films is part of Trident Event Group, which also runs Trident Studios for photography, Trident Music for DJs and Trident Photobooths. Booking several services with one team means one planning call, one shared timeline, and vendors who already know how to work around each other on the day.
