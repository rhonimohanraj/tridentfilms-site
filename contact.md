## Planning a wedding film?

The fastest way to check your date is the inquiry form: https://www.tridentfilms.ca/inquiry.html. Include your wedding date, venue and city, and anything you already know about the film you want. We will follow up about availability, walk you through our packages and answer your questions.

To talk it through, book a 30-minute call with Joey, our Sales Manager: https://www.tridentfilms.ca/book.html

## Where we film

Winnipeg and surrounding communities (Headingley, Oak Bluff, Selkirk, Steinbach); Brandon and the Westman region; Regina and the rest of Saskatchewan; destination weddings.
