Winnipeg wedding video FAQ.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Winnipeg?

Trident Films' Essential Collection costs $4,399 plus GST. That includes 10 hours of coverage, two videographers, a 1-minute teaser, a 5 to 6 minute highlight film, and the full ceremony and toasts films. You hold your date with a $1,500 retainer and pay the rest in two equal instalments before the wedding. Venues outside the city may add travel.

How long until we receive our wedding film?

Expect your films 16 to 20 weeks after your wedding date. That time goes into editing, colour grading, cleaning up ceremony and speech audio, and scoring each film with licensed music. Everything arrives together in one private online gallery, and we let you know early if a delivery ever needs more time.

Do you film at The Fort Garry Hotel?

Yes. The Fort Garry Hotel is one of the Winnipeg venues we know well, from the limestone exterior to the chandeliers of the Crystal Ballroom. We plan ceremony audio with the hotel's event team and time portraits for when the window light is at its best. Our Winnipeg venue guide covers it in more detail.

Is drone footage allowed at Winnipeg venues?

Sometimes. Much of Winnipeg sits inside the controlled airspace around Winnipeg Richardson International Airport, where a drone flight needs an advanced pilot certificate and permission from NAV CANADA. Parks and heritage sites can have their own rules too. Venues just outside the city, like golf courses and rural properties, are usually simpler, and we check every location first.

Do you travel outside Winnipeg?

Yes. We often film just past the Perimeter, at places like Bridges Golf Course in Starbuck, Bel Acres north of the city and Pineridge Hollow near Oakbank. We also cover Selkirk, Steinbach, Stonewall, Oak Bluff, Headingley and Gimli, and we have a separate page for Brandon and Westman weddings.
